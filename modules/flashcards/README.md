# Flashcards

Turns a note you already wrote into recall questions, quizzes you on them one
at a time, and keeps count of the ones you keep missing.

Say any of these and it runs:

- "quiz me on the cell biology note"
- "make flashcards from this"
- "test me, I have a quiz tomorrow"

Cards are saved next to the note they came from, so they are yours and they are
editable. A card you miss three times gets flagged, because a card that keeps
failing usually means the note behind it is unclear.

**What it adds:** one skill. No scripts, no hooks, nothing that runs on its
own.
