# Reading log

Keeps one running list of what you are reading, what you finished, and what you
thought of it.

Say any of these and it runs:

- "I started reading X"
- "I finished X, it was..."
- "add this to my reading list"
- "what have I read this year"

Everything lands in `30-resources/reading-log.md` in your brain folder. Ratings
stay empty until you give one; it never scores a book for you.

**What it adds:** one skill. No scripts, no hooks, nothing that runs on its
own.
